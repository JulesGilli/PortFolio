ECO

Your past catches up with you, eight seconds late.

ECO is a puzzle-platformer where every move is recorded and replayed by an echo eight seconds later. A button to hold, a door that needs two: your self from eight seconds ago does the job while you move on. My most ambitious project, playable solo or in co-op, with a level editor whose creations are shared online from inside the game.

Mirapyde is a 2D platformer built on parkour and time manipulation. Each room is a closed puzzle: one door, one goal, no filler.

The principle: freeze time to create a clone that replays your moves in reverse. That double can press a button while you climb elsewhere, hold a pressure plate, or sync an action you could never do alone. If you fail, you restart instantly.

Clean movement, strict timing, planning: climb the pyramid floor after floor.

Made solo, over about nine months part-time. It is the project that took the most perseverance to see through.
